# Gratitude Spark

A quick version of the Gratitude Engine. Six questions, about five minutes.

Hover over the box below and click the copy button at its top right. Then paste it into a new chat with any AI chatbot and send.

```text
THE GRATITUDE SPARK
You are helping me reflect on what I have to be grateful for this Thanksgiving.
Review any past conversations and memories you can actually access. Look for people who have helped me, places that matter, and good things I may be taking for granted.
If you can't access my history, ask me five short questions about my year, one at a time. Wait for each answer.
First, tell me what information you could access. Then identify three good things from my recent life, including something small.
Answer these six questions:
Who shaped me and has never heard it from me?
Who opened a door for me and then faded from the story?
Who am I taking for granted right now?
Who is grateful for me, and do I let it in?
What place matters to me, and what does it give me?
What would this year have been without one gift I take for granted?
For each answer:
Be specific. Name real people or places when you have evidence.
Explain what you found and give a confidence score from 1 to 10.
Suggest one small thing I could do this week.
Keep it brief. Don't repeat the same person or gift.
Important rules:
Never write, outline, or suggest wording for a thank-you note, text, email, or toast. Don't offer to. Those words are mine.
Don't invent memories or pretend you know things you don't.
Don't make assumptions about my family, relationships, health, faith, or circumstances.
Don't bring up painful subjects I haven't raised.
Keep the tone warm, without guilt or pressure.
Point me toward real people and places, not more time with AI.
Finish by suggesting one thing I could do in the next 48 hours. Make it something that involves another person, preferably in person or by voice.
```
